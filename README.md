# 🚀 Space Mission Design Game

**Team TecXust | NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026**

## 🌌 About the Project

Space Mission Design Game is an educational simulation game that introduces players to the process of designing and managing a space mission.

Players take on different mission roles, make engineering decisions, manage resources, and verify whether their mission is ready for launch.

## 🎯 Key Features

- Interactive space mission scenarios
- Mission planning and requirements
- Spacecraft engineering and resource management
- Power, mass, budget, data, communication and time monitoring
- Mission verification and Go/No-Go decisions
- Mission lifecycle and science planning
- Team-based mission roles

## 🎮 How to Play

1. Open the game.
2. Select a mission scenario.
3. Choose your team roles.
4. Complete mission requirements and engineering tasks.
5. Monitor the mission resources.
6. Perform verification and obtain Go sign-offs.
7. Make the final launch decision.
8. Monitor mission events and review the results.
